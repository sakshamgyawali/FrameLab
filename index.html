Frequently asked

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Your original photo stays on your device; background removal runs in your browser using the Framelab editor.

Do I need an account?

No account or sign-up is needed to open the editor and create a composition.

What format can I export?

Save your composition as a PNG at your original image dimensions.
